About Tracy

Tracy is a student-focused web application designed to help users understand and manage academic attendance information. It provides attendance calculations, subject-wise analysis, timetable-based predictions, and academic planning tools.

What Tracy Does

  • Processes attendance reports imported by the user.
  • Calculates subject-wise and overall attendance percentages.
  • Provides attendance and timetable-based predictions.
  • Provides manual attendance and CGPA calculation tools.
  • Provides timetable information and selected campus-related information.

Local-First Design

Attendance information imported into Tracy is processed in your browser and stored locally on your device for the application's functionality. Tracy does not require you to create an account to use its core attendance features.

Independent Service

Tracy is an independent student utility and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially associated with any university or educational institution unless explicitly stated on the relevant page.

Accuracy

Tracy is intended as a convenience and planning tool. Always verify attendance, timetable, academic, and institutional information against the official records or notices provided by your institution.

Tracy does not require you to upload your attendance Excel file to a Tracy-operated server.
