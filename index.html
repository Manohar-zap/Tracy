About Us

Tracy is a free attendance tracking and prediction tool built for college students. We help you monitor subject-wise attendance, predict future percentages using your timetable, and calculate safe bunks before you skip class.

Our Mission

To give students a simple, privacy-first way to stay on top of attendance requirements without relying on complex spreadsheets.

What We Offer

  • Excel-based attendance import from your college ERP
  • Subject-wise calculators and prediction engine
  • Timetable-based forecasting for current and next week
  • CGPA planning tools

Data Privacy

All your attendance data is stored locally in your browser. We do not upload your personal attendance records to any server.
