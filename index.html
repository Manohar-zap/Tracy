How to Get Attendance Excel

  1. Log in to your college ERP / attendance portal.
  2. Go to Reports → Attendance Summary (or similar).
  3. Select your term/batch and click Export to Excel.
  4. Download the .xlsx file and drop it in Tracy.

Your file should look like this (sample):

Student Name: John Doe  |  Roll No: 21CS001
Course Name Total — Attended (AHDL)
Mathematics (MA101) 40 32
Database Systems (CS201) 38 30
Data Structures (CS202) 42 35

Tracy reads the Course Name, Total, and attended columns automatically.

How to Get Daily Attendance Excel

  1. From your ERP, open Daily Attendance Report.
  2. Pick a date range and export as .xlsx.
  3. Make sure a Date column and Hour 1, Hour 2… columns exist.
  4. Select your class timetable first, then upload here.

Sample format:

Date Hour 1 Hour 2 Hour 3 Hour 4
03/06/2025 P A P P
04/06/2025 P P P A

P = Present, A = Absent. Hours are mapped to subjects via your selected timetable.

About Us

Tracy is a free attendance tracking and prediction tool built for college students. We help you monitor subject-wise attendance, predict future percentages using your timetable, and calculate safe bunks before you skip class.

Our Mission

To give students a simple, privacy-first way to stay on top of attendance requirements without relying on complex spreadsheets.

What We Offer

  • Excel-based attendance import from your college ERP
  • Subject-wise calculators and prediction engine
  • Timetable-based forecasting for current and next week
  • CGPA planning tools

Data Privacy

All your attendance data is stored locally in your browser. We do not upload your personal attendance records to any server.
